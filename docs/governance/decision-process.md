## Purpose

Define how decisions become accepted and how disagreements or missing evidence
are handled.

## Decision Classes

| Decision | Record | Approver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product requirement | Product requirement record | Product owner or maintainers |
| Architectural boundary | ADR, normally supported by a review | Architecture owner or maintainers |
| Local implementation | Code review or plan | Area owner |
| Operational emergency | Incident record and follow-up | Incident lead, then retrospective review |

## Proposal And Review

1. State one decision-shaped question.
2. Identify the accountable owner and affected stakeholders.
3. Gather evidence proportional to the decision's reversibility and impact.
4. Record alternatives and consequences.
5. Record the decision in the appropriate authoritative artifact.
6. Update current documentation and communicate the result.

## Resolving Disagreement

Describe escalation, tie-breaking, and how dissenting evidence is preserved.

## Urgent Decisions

Describe which decisions may be made during an incident and when they must be
reviewed afterward.

## Reopening Decisions

Use observable evidence or changed constraints. Preserve earlier records and
supersede them explicitly rather than rewriting history.
